Website Redesign

Sarah Wilson
July 22, 2026
Updated on July 23, 2026

 

Backstory:

Sulema created her own website using Canva. Was it bad?... Not exactly. It wasn't very aligned with her actual design aesthetic or how she wanted to present herself to clients. It also looked really bad on mobile devices. Most visitors to your website are on their phones. The site needed a complete overhaul. She's worked on high profile projects, her website should reflect that.

The original website was simply a contact form with a few images. I upgraded the site to also include a portfolio and photo gallery for some feature projects that really showcased her expertise while giving clients an idea of what it looks like to work with her. I gave it a much cleaner aesthetic and layout as well as a mobile optimized design. Instead of being embarrassed to send clients to her website she can feel proud that her new site reflects her professionalism.
